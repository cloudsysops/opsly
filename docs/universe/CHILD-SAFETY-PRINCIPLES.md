# CHILD SAFETY PRINCIPLES

- No open adult-child DMs.
- No unrestricted text or voice chat.
- No behavioral advertising.
- No public real identities by default.
- No automatic publishing of a child's work or profile.
- No selling access to children.
- No precise location exposure by default.
- Parent or guardian consent gates sensitive actions.
- Verified Guides provide structured help, not free-form access.
