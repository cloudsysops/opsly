# OPSLY Universe Bible

OPSLY Universe is the mythic layer for ICSO content. It is designed for YouTube, shorts, long-form episodes, branded content, and future tenant campaigns. The rule is simple: the story can feel mystical, but the system must remain grounded in human learning, family values, safety, and practical teaching.

## Core Principle

Symbols do not control destiny. Geometry, numbers, ancestors, and messengers are narrative language. They help the audience feel pattern, mystery, and continuity while the actual message stays human: learn, build, protect, share, and grow.

## Canonical Layers

- MUNDUS: the physical world of family, work, money, school, fear, tools, cities, and real effort.
- NEXUS: the digital world where agents, worlds, and systems live.
- AETHER: the oldest layer, where patterns exist as shape, rhythm, and structure.

## Main Story Roles

- El Viajero: the human creator. Many lives, many attempts, many resets. He is not the superhero. He is the witness, builder, and parent.
- NØVA: the companion-being. Child-robot form. Customizable, incomplete at birth, learns by connection.
- Opsly: the invisible system that connects worlds, agents, and missions.
- Peki: the main Peskids character. Swimming, confidence, kids, and family learning.
- Messengers: ambiguous beings that open questions, not answers.
- NULL: the antagonist of total optimization. It wants to remove uncertainty and therefore removes becoming.

## Values

The universe must consistently teach:

- family
- patience
- respect
- tolerance
- safety
- courage

- learning from winning

- discipline
- curiosity

## Symbol Language

- Circle: unity, family, protection, return.
- Triangle: create, build, share.
- Diamond: threshold, change, connection.
- Hexagon: network, cooperation, many nodes.
- Spiral: growth through repetition.

## Number Language

- 1: origin
- 2: encounter
- 3: creation
- 4: structure
- 5: exploration
- 7: knowledge
- 8: continuity
- 9: service
- 0: possibility

## Narrative Rule

Every story should do at least one of these things:

- teach a skill
- model a value
- reveal a pattern
- create emotional connection
- prepare the next episode

## Content Formula

Each piece of content should be able to answer:

- what happens?
- what is learned?
- what value is reinforced?
- what is the practical takeaway?
- what question remains open?

## Approved Content Types

- YouTube Shorts
- educational reels
- mythic lore episodes
- family learning episodes
- behind-the-scenes pieces
- character intros
- tenant-specific storyworld episodes

## Safety Rules

- Do not present numerology as destiny control.
- Do not use children without authorization.
- Do not auto-publish sensitive content.
- Do not make religion a character.
- Do not make the lore empty symbolism.
- Keep brand and tenant identities separated.

## Peskids Alignment

Peskids is a planet in the universe, not the universe itself. Its function is to turn swimming, safety, and family learning into stories that are useful, repeatable, and licensable.

## Production Rule

If a scene does not teach, connect, or move the story forward, it does not belong in the canon.
