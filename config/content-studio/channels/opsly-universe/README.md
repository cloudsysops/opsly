# OPSLY Universe — YouTube Shorts

This channel is the story surface for the Opsly Universe: worlds, characters,
missions and games. It is not Peskids content and it does not use Peskids
student or family data.

The visual direction is cinematic illustrated science-fantasy: deep blue and
violet space, turquoise portals, warm gold light, expressive robot companions,
and readable Spanish captions. Characters must remain consistent with the
Universe canon; the render agent may vary camera, lighting and setting, but may
not invent a new identity or backstory.

The PC-gamer is the only render host. The Mac creates/queues jobs, syncs the
result and prepares the YouTube upload kit. Human review is required before
any external publication.
