**For:** Barbers, Restaurant Owners, Construction Managers, Freelancers, Local Service Businesses

## What You Get (In Plain English)

### The Problem You Have Now
- You spend **2-4 hours per week** on manual follow-ups (emails, texts, calls)
- You forget to reach out to clients sometimes
- Clients get forgotten for weeks → they go to a competitor
- You have no system to track who you've contacted

### What Opsly Does
Opsly watches your client interactions and **reminds you who to follow up with**. When it's time:

1. **Opsly drafts a message** for you (based on real context about the client)
2. **You read it in 5 seconds** and hit "Send" or "Edit"
3. **Opsly sends it** via email, WhatsApp, or SMS
4. **You track results** in a simple dashboard

**That's it.**
