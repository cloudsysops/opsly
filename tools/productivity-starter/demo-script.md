# Opsly Productivity Demo — 15-Minute Sales Script

**Target:** Barbershop/Restaurant/Construction/Freelance owner struggling with manual client follow-ups

**Goal:** Show how Opsly automates routine work while owner stays 100% in control

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist (5 min before)
- [ ] Open Gmail (show a real inbox, messy okay)
- [ ] Open Notion (show a client list or project list)
- [ ] Open WhatsApp Web
- [ ] Have a sample client follow-up ready to demo
- [ ] Screenshare settings tested

---

## Part 1: The Problem (1 min)

**You say:**
> "How much time do you spend each week on follow-ups? Emails to clients, reminders, confirmations, checking who's been contacted?"

**Listen.** Let them answer. (They'll say 1-5 hours.)

> "Yeah. And it's always the same work, right? 'Hey, just checking in about your appointment,' 'Did you see the quote I sent?' That kind of thing."

**Don't pitch yet. Just mirror their pain.**

---

## Part 2: The Solution (2 min)

**You say:**
> "What if Opsly could draft those follow-ups FOR you, organize them, and send them only when YOU say yes? No hidden automation. You see every message before it goes out."

**Key phrase:** "You see, approve, Opsly sends."

---

## Part 3: Live Demo (10 min)

### 3A. Show Gmail Setup (2 min)
**Narrate:**
> "Here's your Gmail. You receive a lot of emails. Opsly watches for patterns—like client inquiry that's been sitting 3 days without a response. Opsly doesn't auto-delete or auto-send anything. Instead, it creates a smart label."

**Show:**
- Gmail inbox (real or sample)
- A label called `Review-Follow-Up` (auto-created by Opsly)
- Point out: "Opsly sorted this. YOU still decide what to do."

**Do NOT say:** "Machine learning detected" or "AI algorithm analyzed." Just: "Opsly organized this for you."

---

### 3B. Show Approval Workflow (4 min)

**Narrate:**
> "Here's where the magic happens. Opsly drafted a follow-up message based on this client's history."

**Open Notion or a simple web interface showing:**
```
Client: Maria (Haircut appointment 3 days ago)
Opsly's Draft Message:
"Hi Maria! Just checking in—did your haircut turn out great? 
Let us know if you need anything else! 💇‍♀️"

[Preview]  [Approve] [Edit] [Skip]
```

**You say:**
> "You can read it, edit it, or skip it. Only when you hit Approve does Opsly actually send it. No surprises."

**Click [Approve].** Show it send via WhatsApp.

**Narrate:**
> "Boom. One message, 10 seconds from you. Without Opsly, you'd spend 5 minutes typing, finding her contact, copying/pasting. 100 clients × 5 min = 500 min/month. That's 8+ hours just on follow-ups."

---

### 3C. Show Results (2 min)

**Open Notion or dashboard:**
- "Messages sent this week: 23"
- "Client responses: 8"
- "New appointments booked: 3"

**You say:**
> "That's real ROI. Opsly doesn't just save time—it brings in business."

---

### 3D. Show Safety & Control (2 min)

**Narrate:**
> "You keep 100% control. Anytime you want to pause Opsly, change a message, or just do it manually—you can. Opsly never sends anything without your approval."

**Show one more thing:**
- A toggled-off automation (shows user can disable)
- A message you *edited* before approval (shows you can customize)

**You say:**
> "This isn't a robot taking over. This is YOU, but faster."

---

## Part 4: Close (2 min)

**You say:**
> "So here's what we offer: 30-day free trial. You set up 2-3 automations. You see if it's worth your time. If it's not, we part as friends. No contract, no surprises."

**Pause.**

> "What's one client follow-up you do every single week that you'd love to automate?"

**Listen.** They'll tell you. Then say:

> "Let's set that one up first. Takes 5 minutes, and you'll see the value immediately."

---

## Common Objections (Keep Answers Simple)

**Q: "What if the AI gets it wrong?"**
> "It can't send anything without you. You approve every message. If it's wrong, you just skip it."

**Q: "Will it cost a lot?"**
> "Nope. $29/month, and it pays for itself in the first week of time saved. Plus—new appointments from better follow-ups."

**Q: "What if my clients think it's automated?"**
> "The message is personal and you control it. Clients have no idea—they just get a friendly follow-up and respond."

**Q: "Does Opsly see my client data?"**
> "Only to help you—and only what you approve. Your data stays yours. We're like a trusted assistant who keeps her mouth shut."

---

## Demo Cleanup

- [ ] Close all tabs except what you showed
- [ ] Don't mention technical terms (API, webhook, orchestrator, etc.)
- [ ] If they ask "how," say: "It's built so you don't have to think about it."
- [ ] Focus on TIME SAVED and NEW REVENUE
- [ ] Always emphasize: "You stay in control"

---

## Success Metrics for This Demo
- [ ] Client says "I want to try it"
- [ ] Client has 1-2 ideas of what to automate
- [ ] Client asks about timeline (not "will it work")
- [ ] Client asks about cost (not "is this legit")

---

## What NOT to Show
- ❌ Code or technical architecture
- ❌ "AI explained" or machine learning terminology
- ❌ Backend databases or servers
- ❌ Anything that looks like manual coding
- ❌ Safety disclaimers (mention naturally, not prominently)

---

## What TO Emphasize
- ✅ Time saved (actual hours per week)
- ✅ Revenue impact (new appointments, better response rates)
- ✅ User control (you approve everything)
- ✅ Simplicity (no training needed)
- ✅ Risk-free trial (30 days, no contract)
