# Opsly Productivity Demo Package

**15-minute sales pitch for non-technical business owners**

This package contains everything you need to sell Opsly Productivity to a barber, restaurant owner, construction manager, or freelancer.

---

## 📋 What's Inside

### 1. **offer.md** — The Value Proposition
Read this first. It's what you send/read BEFORE the demo.

- **Who it's for:** Busy business owners (barbershops, restaurants, construction, freelance)
- **What problem it solves:** Manual follow-ups eating 2-4 hours/week
- **What the ROI is:** Save 8+ hours/month, book 1-3 new appointments/month
- **Pricing:** $29/month, 30-day free trial, no contracts

**How to use it:**
- Email it to prospects before a call
- Read it aloud during discovery call (if they seem confused)
- Use examples from it (barbershop, restaurant, construction, freelancer)

---

### 2. **demo-script.md** — What To Say
Talking points for your 15-minute Zoom demo.

- Part 1: Establish the problem (1 min)
- Part 2: Present the solution (2 min)
- Part 3: Live demo (10 min)
- Part 4: Close (2 min)

**How to use it:**
- Read it once before the call (internalize, don't memorize)
- Have it open on a second monitor during the demo
- Follow the structure (Problem → Solution → Demo → Close)

---

### 3. **DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md** — Step-By-Step
Detailed, click-by-click guide for the live demo.

- Pre-demo setup checklist
- Exact screens to show
- What to say at each step
- Troubleshooting guide
- Objection responses
- Follow-up email template

**How to use it:**
- Reference it during the demo if you get stuck
- Use the objection responses verbatim
- Send the follow-up email within 1 hour of demo
- Use the Day-7 check-in script

---

## 🎯 Quick Start (First Demo)

### Day Before Demo
1. Read **offer.md**
2. Skim **demo-script.md**
3. Check **DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md** Pre-Demo Setup

### 1 Hour Before Demo
1. Follow the Pre-Demo Setup Checklist (DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md)
2. Have sample data ready (client inbox, draft message, results view)
3. Test screenshare

### During Demo (15 min)
1. **Opening (1 min):** Ask about their current follow-up process
2. **Problem (1 min):** Show their messy inbox
3. **Solution (2 min):** Explain the workflow
4. **Demo (6 min):** Show draft → approve → send
5. **Control (3 min):** Show safety + audit trail
6. **Customize (2 min):** Ask what they want to automate
7. **Close (1 min):** Offer 30-day free trial

### After Demo (Within 1 Hour)
- Send the follow-up email (see DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md)
- Schedule Day-7 check-in

## 💡 Key Principles

### This Demo Works Because:

✅ **Starts with their problem** — "How much time do you spend on follow-ups?"

✅ **Shows, doesn't tell** — Live demo of draft → approve → send

✅ **Emphasizes control** — "You approve every message"

✅ **Focuses on ROI** — Time saved + new revenue

✅ **No tech jargon** — Works for anyone, not just engineers

✅ **Zero pressure** — 30-day free trial, cancel anytime

✅ **Built for 15 minutes** — Fits into a lunch meeting

### Things This Demo Does NOT Do:

❌ Use words like "AI," "algorithm," "API," "machine learning"

❌ Show code, architecture, or technical details

❌ Make promises Opsly can't keep

❌ Use fear or urgency ("Act now!" "Limited spots!")

❌ Overcomplicate the value prop

❌ Forget to ask for the sale

## 📞 Target Customers

### This Works Great For:
- 💇 **Barbershop owner** — Follow up after haircuts, rebook regulars
- 🍕 **Restaurant owner** — Remind regulars, follow up after reviews
- 🏗️ **Construction manager** — Follow up on bids, milestone reminders
- 💼 **Freelancer** — Follow up on proposals, invoice reminders
- 🏥 **Dentist/hygienist** — Appointment reminders, follow-up cleanings
- 🏋️ **Fitness trainer** — Client check-ins, retention follow-ups

### Avoid Selling To (For Now):
- Enterprise sales (they need longer cycle, different pricing)
- SaaS companies (they have different needs)
- Anyone with <$50k/month revenue (can't afford it)

## 📊 Success Metrics

**Demo went well if:**
- ✅ They asked at least 2 questions
- ✅ They said yes to free trial
- ✅ They told you 2-3 things they want to automate
- ✅ They asked about cost (not "does it work?")
- ✅ They mentioned a specific pain point

**Conversion metrics to track:**
- Demo-to-trial: Aim for 70% (7 out of 10 demos = trial signup)
- Trial-to-paid: Aim for 50% (5 out of 10 trials = paid customer)
- Overall: 35% demo-to-paid (3-4 out of 10 demos = paying customer by Day 30)

## 🔐 Safety & Compliance Notes

**Important reminders:**
- ✅ Never promise data deletion faster than it actually happens
- ✅ Never claim Opsly can integrate with systems we haven't tested
- ✅ Always emphasize user approval and control
- ✅ Don't claim we're SOC2 or HIPAA if we aren't (yet)
- ✅ Be honest about limitations (no auto-scheduling, no auto-delete)

---

## 📞 Getting Help

**If customer asks:**
- "How much data can we handle?" → "Thousands of clients, unlimited messages"
- "What if I have privacy concerns?" → Direct to our Privacy Policy + offer phone call with founder
- "Can we white-label this?" → "Not in the Starter plan, but let's talk about future plans"
- "What if Opsly makes a mistake?" → "You approve every message, so you catch it before it sends"

---

## 🎓 Training Checklist

Before your first demo, confirm you can:

- [ ] Explain the problem in under 1 minute
- [ ] Show a draft → approve → send flow
- [ ] Answer "Why not just do it myself?" without sounding defensive
- [ ] Calculate ROI on the fly (hours saved × hourly rate)
- [ ] Describe what Opsly can't do (auto-delete, auto-schedule, etc.)
- [ ] Describe what you stay in control of (approvals, edits, audit trail)
- [ ] Close with "30-day free trial, cancel anytime"
- [ ] Schedule Day-7 follow-up before they leave the call
