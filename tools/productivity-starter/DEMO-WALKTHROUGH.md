# Opsly Productivity Demo — Step-By-Step Walkthrough

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Tools:** Gmail, Notion (or simple web view), WhatsApp Web
**Audience:** Non-technical business owner

---

## Pre-Demo Setup (Do This 10 Minutes Before)

### 1. Prepare Your Screen
- [ ] Close all tabs except: Gmail, Notion/Dashboard, WhatsApp Web
- [ ] Have a notepad ready to jot down what they want to automate
- [ ] Test screenshare (audio + video)
- [ ] Brightness/contrast adjusted so they can see clearly

### 2. Have Sample Data Ready
- [ ] One real or realistic client inbox (messy is fine, shows real problem)
- [ ] One draft follow-up message visible
- [ ] One approval workflow visible
- [ ] One "results" view (shows 3-5 sent messages, 1-2 responses)

### 3. Have Their Business Context
- [ ] Know how many clients they have (approximate)
- [ ] Know one specific pain point (from pre-call research)
- [ ] Know one follow-up they do every week

---

## Part 1: Opening (1 minute)

**You on video:**
> "Hi [Name]! Great to meet you. Before we dive in, quick question: How are you currently staying in touch with clients? Like, how do you remember to follow up with them?"

**Let them talk.** (They'll say: email, text, spreadsheet, just remember, etc.)

**You respond:**
> "Got it. And how much time does that take you each week?"

**Listen. Write down their answer.** (This is your hook later.)

**You:**
> "Okay, so that's [their number] hours that could be back in your pocket. Let me show you how Opsly saves that time—and actually brings in more business."

---

## Part 2: The Problem (Screenshare) (1 minute)

**Open Gmail.**

**You say:**
> "So here's your inbox right now. You've got a mix of client messages, follow-ups you need to do, stuff that's falling through cracks. Does this look familiar?"

**Point to 2-3 emails.**

> "Maria's email from 3 days ago—no response yet. This quote went out last week, haven't heard back. This is the work that eats your time, right?"

**Do NOT:** Point out all problems. Just establish: "This is messy, and it's your problem."

---

## Part 3: The Solution Overview (2 minutes)

**You say:**
> "Here's what Opsly does. It watches your inbox. When it sees patterns—like a client who hasn't responded in 3 days, or an appointment coming up—it **drafts a message for you**."

**Pause.**

> "But—and this is the key part—it doesn't send anything. You review it, approve it, edit it, or skip it. You stay 100% in control."

**You say:**
> "Then, when you approve, Opsly sends it through email, WhatsApp, or text. And we track results so you can see what's actually working."

---

## Part 4: Live Demo — Approval Workflow (6 minutes)

### Step 1: Show a Draft Message (1 min)

**Switch to Notion or simple dashboard view.**

**Show a screen like this:**
```
[Opsly Productivity]

CLIENT: Maria Rodriguez
Last interaction: 3 days ago (Haircut appointment)
Status: No follow-up yet

📝 DRAFT MESSAGE:

Subject: How did your haircut turn out? 💇‍♀️

"Hi Maria!

I hope you loved your cut! I'd love to hear how it's looking. 
Let me know if you need anything else.

Can't wait to see you again!
— [Your Name]"

[Preview]  [Approve]  [Edit]  [Skip]
```

**You say:**
> "Opsly drafted this based on Maria's history—she had a haircut 3 days ago. Is this something you'd send to her?"

**Let them answer.** Usually: "Yeah, but I'd change [X]" — perfect.

### Step 2: Show the Edit Capability (1 min)

**You say:**
> "Easy. Click Edit, change it to whatever you want."

**Click [Edit]. Type a quick change:**

Change: "I'd love to hear how it's looking" → "I'd love to hear how YOU like it"

**Click [Save].**

**You say:**
> "Now it's exactly how you'd say it. When you're ready, hit Approve."

### Step 3: Send It (1 min)

**Click [Approve].**

**Show a quick "Sending..." loader, then:**

```
✅ Message sent to Maria via WhatsApp
Sent: Today at 2:15 PM

Waiting for response...
```

**You say:**
> "Done. One message, 10 seconds from you. Without Opsly, you're spending 5 minutes typing, finding her contact, sending it manually. Times 100 clients, times 10+ follow-ups... that's hours every month."

### Step 4: Show Results Dashboard (2 min)

**Click to a "This Week" or "This Month" view:**

```
[Opsly Results — This Month]

Messages drafted: 23
Messages sent: 21
Client responses: 8
New appointments booked: 3

Recent responses:
📱 Maria: "Loved it! Can I book for next month?"
📱 David: "It looks great, thanks!"
📱 Jasmine: "Can you fit me in Tuesday?"
```

**You say:**
> "This is real. Maria responded and booked her next appointment. David engaged. Jasmine is a new booking. That's revenue. All because you followed up."

**Pause.**

> "If even half your clients respond like this, that's [1-2 new appointments per week]. At your average ticket price, that's... [calculate out loud]."

**Let the math do the selling.**

---

## Part 5: Show Safety & Control (3 minutes)

### What You CAN Do:

**You say:**
> "You have full control. You can..."

**Click through these one by one:**

1. **Edit before sending** (already showed)
2. **Skip a draft** → Click [Skip] → "This one's not right, skip it"
3. **Turn off an automation** → Show toggle: "Auto follow-up: ON/OFF"
4. **See every message sent** → Show audit log: "Maria, 3/15, 2:15 PM, WhatsApp, Message: [exact text]"

**You say:**
> "Complete transparency. You can see exactly what was sent, when, to whom. No surprises."

### What You CANNOT Do (We protect you):

**You say:**
> "Opsly never does these things:
> - Auto-delete your emails
> - Automatically schedule messages without you
> - Send anything without your approval
> - Access anything you don't show it
> - Keep data after you leave

You stay in control. Period."

---

## Part 6: Customize for THEM (2 minutes)

**You say:**
> "So here's what we'd set up for you specifically. Earlier you said you spend [their number] hours on follow-ups. What's one follow-up you do every single week that you'd love to not think about?"

**Let them answer.** (They'll say something like: "Reminding clients about their appointment," "Following up on quotes," "Thanking new clients," etc.)

**You:**
> "Perfect. That's automation #1. What's another?"

**Get 2-3 ideas from them.**

**You:**
> "Great. We'd set those three up, and in the first week, you'd see about [estimate] follow-ups drafted. You'd approve them, hit send, and watch new appointments come in."

---

## Part 7: Close (1 minute)

**You say:**
> "So here's what I'm offering:
> - 30 days completely free
> - We set up those 2-3 automations for you
> - You use Opsly for 30 days
> - If it's worth your time, it's $29/month
> - If not, no harm, no contract

What do you think?"

**If YES:**
> "Let's get you set up right now. Takes 5 minutes, and you'll see your first drafts by tomorrow morning. Sound good?"

**If "Let me think about it":**
> "Fair. Can we schedule 15 minutes next week to check in? By then you'll have had time to think about what you'd automate, and I can answer any other questions."

---

## Live Demo Troubleshooting

### If WiFi Cuts Out
- Have a pre-recorded 2-minute video of the workflow (play that)
- Switch to audio + share screenshots on Zoom

### If They Ask "How Does It Work?" (Technical)
**You say:**
> "Honestly, the technical part doesn't matter. What matters is: you approve every message, Opsly sends it, you see results. We handle the tech so you don't have to think about it."

**Don't explain AI, algorithms, webhooks, etc.**

### If They Seem Skeptical
**You say:**
> "I get it. Try it free for 30 days. Zero risk. Use it, see the time saved, see the new appointments. Then decide. That's the fairest way to know if it's for you."

### If They Ask "What If You Mess Up?"
**You say:**
> "We can't. You approve every message before it sends. If we draft something bad, you just skip it. You're the final gatekeeper."

## Post-Demo (Immediately After)

**You say:**
> "That was great. Quick recap: You'd save [their number] hours/week, likely get [1-3] new appointments/month, it costs $29/month, and you have 30 days free to try. Should we get you started?"

**If YES:**
1. Send them onboarding email (immediately)
2. Schedule their first check-in for Day 7 (to see how it's going)

**If NO:**
1. Ask: "What would make this a yes?"
2. Listen. Address the concern.
3. If still no: "Okay, when can we circle back?"

**Call/message them:**
> "Hey! Just checking in—how's Opsly going? Are the follow-ups showing up? Any questions?"

**This is key.** Most dropoff happens Day 1-7. A quick check-in keeps them engaged.

## Key Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Live demo beats explanation.
2. **Let them talk.** Listening builds trust.
3. **Do the math.** [Hours saved] × [their hourly rate] = [value].
4. **Emphasize control.** "You approve" is the whole pitch.
5. **Make it easy.** "30 days free, cancel anytime" removes friction.
6. **Follow up.** Day-1 email, Day-7 check-in = 70% conversion.
