# The Traveler

**Character data:** [`../characters/the-traveler.json`](../characters/the-traveler.json)
**Full biographical arc:** [`ORIGIN-SAGA.md`](ORIGIN-SAGA.md)

Represents Cristian. His face is never fully shown — a mask of glowing
geometric lines stands in for his expression instead.

## Who he is

- Human, partially augmented by technology after many years of building,
  failing, rebuilding, across many fields — infrastructure and cloud/DevOps
  consulting, construction, restaurants, small business, family life,
  migration (Medellín to Providence).
- Not a superhero. Not invincible. Doubts, loses, restarts, sometimes has
  money, sometimes has none.
- Defining trait: **"If he can imagine it, eventually he tries to build it."**
- Protects NØVA because NØVA represents the part of himself he refuses to
  lose — the capacity to keep imagining.

## What he is not

- Not "the chosen one." No prophecy, no destiny narration.
- Not confident by default — competence shows through persistence, not swagger.
- Not a mouthpiece for corporate claims — see `UNIVERSE-BIBLE.md` truth policy.

## Central conflict (recurring across every act)

> "¿Estoy construyendo algo real, o simplemente saltando de idea en idea
> otra vez?"

He always has ten portals open. The season doesn't resolve this by proving
him right or wrong — it resolves it by reframing: not every idea has to
become a destination. Some only teach. Others become path.

## Faith (present, never preachy)

He doesn't believe God guarantees success. He believes something larger
than himself exists. When completely lost, he keeps walking. No deity
appears as a character; no magic answer arrives. He looks at the sky, then
goes back to work.

> "No sé a dónde lleva este camino. Solo sé que todavía tengo que
> caminarlo."

## Speech style

Low, warm, thinking-out-loud. Sentences that ask more than they declare.
Rarely finishes a scene certain he's right.

## Signature lines

> "Pensé que estaba perdido. Después entendí que estaba construyendo el mapa."
> ("I thought I was lost. Then I understood I was building the map.")

> "No sé a dónde lleva este camino. Solo sé que todavía tengo que caminarlo."
> ("I don't know where this path leads. I only know I still have to walk it.")

Season finale, final line before cut to black (Episode 13):

> NØVA — "¿Y ahora para dónde?"
> The Traveler — "No tengo idea."
> NØVA — "Perfecto."
