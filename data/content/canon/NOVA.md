# NØVA

**Character data:** [`../characters/nova.json`](../characters/nova.json)

NØVA is not simply The Traveler's assistant — NØVA is a symbolic
representation of his inner child: curious, unafraid, imaginative, always
asking "but what if it actually works?"

## Relationship to The Traveler

The Traveler brings experience. NØVA brings imagination. Neither works fully
without the other — where The Traveler sees a budget problem, NØVA sees a
possibility. The Traveler protects NØVA's optimism; NØVA keeps The Traveler
from calcifying into pure caution.

> The Traveler: "Eso no se puede hacer."
> NØVA: "¿Por qué?"
> The Traveler: "Porque necesitaríamos dinero, personas, servidores..."
> NØVA: "¿Y si empezamos con lo que tenemos?"

## First appearance (canon)

NØVA is not built or manufactured on-screen — NØVA *awakens*, during The
Traveler's first real conversation with an AI, as a small point of blue
light that opens its eyes for the first time. See Season 1, Episode 1 ("The
Question") — chronologically the end of Act I ("Too Many Lives," Episode 2,
which happens *before* this and shows The Traveler still alone).

## On failure

NØVA's first exposure to a failed build (Episode 3) establishes how the two
of them metabolize failure together — not as erasure, but as information:

> NØVA: "¿Fallamos?"
> The Traveler: "Sí."
> NØVA: "¿Entonces la borramos?"
> The Traveler: "No. Ahora sabemos por dónde no pasar."

## On agents

NØVA is never an "agent" or worker in the Opsly sense — briefly worried
about this in Episode 7, reassured that NØVA is companion, not function:

> NØVA: "¿Son robots como yo?"
> The Traveler: "No exactamente. Son partes de una idea aprendiendo a
> trabajar juntas."

## Speech style

Fast, bright, endlessly curious. Asks "why" and "what if" more than any
other character in the universe.

## Signature line

> "¿Pero qué pasaría si sí funciona?"
> ("But what if it actually works?")
