# Messenger

**Character data:** [`../characters/messenger.json`](../characters/messenger.json)
**Merged in from:** `feat/icso-youtube-kids-swim` — see [`CONTINUITY-RULES.md`](CONTINUITY-RULES.md).

A mythic, non-human entity that opens questions rather than answers.
Appears from and dissolves back into AETHER (see [`WORLDS.md`](WORLDS.md)),
visually tied to the same sacred-geometry family as the cipher fragments
(see [`SYMBOLS.md`](SYMBOLS.md)).

## Function

Turning-point device: the Messenger's brief appearances mark moments where
a question opens rather than resolves — never a source of complete
answers. Never has a fixed humanoid body across appearances, never speaks
with an audible voice (communicates only through brief symbol/text
fragments).

## Where used

Not in any already-scripted Season 1 episode — available as a device for
future episodes that need a threshold/revelation beat, particularly ones
extending the symbol-cipher mystery beyond Episode 13's finale.
