# Wavo — Peki Lab Crossover

**Character data:** [`../characters/wavo.json`](../characters/wavo.json) (unchanged from the MVP —
Wavo's design already fits the universe without modification).

Wavo represents the Peskids world: swimming, children, water, learning,
safety, fun. In the Opsly Universe, Peskids is the first real "tenant world"
The Traveler and NØVA explore together — see Season 1, Episode 4 ("Peskids").

## Crossover rule

NØVA and Wavo meet on-screen exactly once in Season 1 (Episode 4). Their
personalities are complementary, not duplicate: NØVA is curious about
*everything*; Wavo is specifically knowledgeable and protective about
*water and swimming*. Keep that boundary — don't let Wavo become a generic
second NØVA, and don't let NØVA start acting as a swimming expert.

No changes to Wavo's visual design, voice, or personality from the original
Character Bible are needed for this crossover.
