Peki is the main Peskids hero: swimming, confidence, and family learning,
built to teach kids directly on-screen.

## Peki vs. Wavo — how they divide roles

Both live on Peskids Planet (see [`WORLDS.md`](WORLDS.md)) and both are
canonical — this is not a duplicate, it's a division of labor:

| | Peki | Wavo |
|---|---|---|
| Function | Teaches swimming/confidence directly to kids | Tech/dashboard/automation sidekick |
| Register | Warm, encouraging, smooth-edged, no visible mechanics | Smart, helpful, visible tech gadgets, goggles |
| Narrative role | Guide (primary on-screen teacher) | Supporter (explain/assist/demo) |
| Where used | Kids-facing swim-skill content | Platform-facing / "how this works" content |
