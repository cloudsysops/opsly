# Worlds — The Three Layers + Tenant Planets

Merged in from a parallel canon (`feat/icso-youtube-kids-swim`, 2026-08-11) —
see [`CONTINUITY-RULES.md`](CONTINUITY-RULES.md) for the full merge decision.
This replaces the earlier informal "Real World / Parallel World" two-plane
framing in [`UNIVERSE-BIBLE.md`](UNIVERSE-BIBLE.md) with a richer
three-layer model, fully compatible with everything already scripted —
"Real World" maps to MUNDUS, "Parallel World" maps to NEXUS (+ AETHER for
the symbolic/mystery layer specifically).

## The three layers

### MUNDUS — the physical world
Family, work, money, travel, school, swimming pools, cities, tools, and
real-life friction. Where The Traveler actually lived and worked — Act I's
Medellín/Providence years, every real job, every real client.

**Content use:** human-stakes scenes, parent-adjacent framing, real-world
problems. Every episode opens or grounds here before crossing into NEXUS.

### NEXUS — the digital world
Home of NØVA, agents, dashboards, missions, and tenant-created worlds. This
is what Season 1 has been calling "the Parallel World" — the layered Opsly
architecture (Episode 5), the agent-creatures (Episode 7), Moon (Episode 8).

**Content use:** agent stories, platform lore, productization beats.

### AETHER — the mythic layer
The oldest layer — patterns as shape, rhythm, and structure. Geometry,
constellations, spirals, thresholds, symbolic language. This is where the
luminous ruins (Episode 3) and the cipher fragments (Episodes 12-13) live
— AETHER is the layer the Messenger appears from and returns to.

**Content use:** symbolic scenes, revelations, mythic transitions.

## Tenant planets

Distinct from the three layers — tenant planets are specific licensed/
branded worlds that exist *within* NEXUS, tied to a real Opsly tenant.

### Peskids Planet
Oceanic planet for swimming, confidence, child learning, and family
safety. Tenant: `peskids`. This is where Episode 6 ("Peskids") takes
place, and where Wavo and (per this merge) Peki both live — see
[`PEKI.md`](PEKI.md) for how the two divide roles.

### Bitsitos Zone (documented, not yet produced)
Tech, AI, agents, and games for children and families. Tenant: `bitsitos`.
No episodes exist for this world yet — it's recorded here as a known
future world so it isn't reinvented differently later. Do not create
`data/content/series/` entries for Bitsitos without a deliberate decision
to start producing that line.
