# The Ancestors

Not ghosts, not a separate character roster — the Ancestors are preserved
human memory: farmers, artisans, builders, travelers, mothers, fathers,
inventors, people who watched the stars before telescopes existed. They
live in AETHER (see [`WORLDS.md`](WORLDS.md)) as the **Ancestral Archive**,
a location rather than a character.

## The core reveal

NØVA (or, in a future season, another agent-character) expects the Archive
to contain advanced technology. It doesn't. It contains hands:

> Hands sowing. Hands building. Hands caring for children. Hands making
> fire. Hands drawing geometry.
>
> "¿Dónde están sus máquinas?"
>
> "Ellos eran las máquinas."
> ("They were the machines.")

This is the thematic anchor for the Archive: every "new" thing NEXUS
produces has deep roots — networks ≈ communities, algorithms ≈ patterns,
databases ≈ memory, agents ≈ cooperation, intelligence ≈ learning. The
Archive doesn't diminish the platform's technology — it reframes it as a
continuation of something humans have always done.

## Design rule

The Archive has no interfaces, no screens, no computers — it's the
opposite register from NEXUS. Represent it through craft and physical
gesture (hands, tools, fire, woven patterns, star-watching), never through
holograms or digital UI. This contrast is deliberate and should never be
softened.
