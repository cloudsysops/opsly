# S01E01 — Las hermanas despiertan

## Purpose
Introduce Arena and Brissa through a shared event, not separate origin stories.

## Canon beats
1. Crystal Temple is damaged before they arrive.
2. Arena awakens to a repair instinct.
3. Brissa awakens to a protection instinct.
4. Their abilities connect instead of competing.
5. They choose to move forward together.
6. A signal from Earth sets up Orion.

## Production note
Prefer real Crystal Temple gameplay for the opening and portal reveal. Character close-ups may be rendered separately, but their appearance must follow Universe canon.

## Closing line
**Arena:** “Si se rompe, se cose.”
