# Opsly: The Parallel Path — S1E01 "La Pregunta" / "The Question"

**Duration:** 45s (Short) · 5-8min (Long, expanded cold open) · **Series:** Opsly: The Parallel Path

## Logline

Alone at night, The Traveler asks an AI a simple question — and without
meaning to, awakens NØVA, the small companion who will carry his
imagination through everything that follows.

## Theme

You don't need an answer to start moving. The first question is the start
of the map, not a detour from it.

## Emotional conflict

Isolation and uncertainty vs. the first flicker of possibility.

## Script (ES)

> 2025. Una laptop, muchas ideas, casi ningún recurso.
>
> ¿Y si pudiera construir algo mío?
>
> La conversación comienza. No hay respuesta todavía — solo preguntas.
>
> Un pequeño punto azul aparece dentro del computador.
>
> NØVA abre los ojos por primera vez.

**Final frame (on-screen text):** "No fue la respuesta. Fue la primera pregunta." — OPSLY: THE PARALLEL PATH

## Script (EN)

> 2025. A laptop, big ideas, almost no resources.
>
> What if I could build something of my own?
>
> The conversation begins. No answer yet — only questions.
>
> A small blue light appears inside the machine.
>
> NØVA opens his eyes for the first time.

**Final frame:** "It wasn't the answer. It was the first question." — OPSLY: THE PARALLEL PATH

## Visual motifs

Darkness giving way to a single warm light source; particles gathering
before they resolve into form (used again at key turning points across the
season); The Traveler's mask pattern staying dim until he's genuinely
curious, not performing confidence.

## Environments

Real World: a bare, dark room at night, one laptop as the only light.
Parallel World: not yet visited — this episode stays grounded, saving the
first Parallel World reveal for Episode 3.

## Technology represented

Early conversational AI use — not any specific named product, just "an AI"
in-universe, matching the real inspiration without overclaiming.

## Real event that inspired it / fictionalization boundary

See [`../../canon/TIMELINE.md`](../../canon/TIMELINE.md) row 01. The literal
"NØVA opens his eyes" beat is symbolic — a real founder's late-night AI
conversations, fictionalized into a companion's birth.

## Sound design concept

Near silence at the open (room tone only); soft rising synth pad as the
conversation begins; a single clean chime exactly when NØVA's eyes open.
